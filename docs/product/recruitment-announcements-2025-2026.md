# Recruitment announcements 2025–2026: facts from the Kinyarwanda originals

**Status:** Reference · extracted 2026-10-25 from the signed Kinyarwanda originals (scans held by the platform owner) · **Authoritative over any English translation**

The English translations first supplied with these announcements contain **material translation errors**: wrong dates, a wrong exam hour, a reversed age limit, wrong provinces, and an invented requirement (§7). Scenario files, campaign policies and tests must cite this document, never the translations. Where an original is itself inconsistent, that is recorded in §8 and not silently corrected.

Kinyarwanda time is counted from 6 a.m. *Saa mbili za mu gitondo* is 08:00, and *saa tatu za mu gitondo* is 09:00.

## 1. RDF: regular personnel and Reserve Force (2026)

*Itangazo ku bifuza kwinjira mu Ngabo z'u Rwanda.* RDF HQs, J1 Department. Signed in Kigali on **15 May 2026** by Col. Lambert SENDEGEYA, Umuyobozi Mukuru Ushinzwe Abakozi.

- **Regular personnel** (*abasirikare bato*), men and women:
  - Rwandan;
  - in good health;
  - **completed S3**;
  - **aged 18–25**;
  - never convicted by a court;
  - not on the list of persons dismissed from public service;
  - of good conduct;
  - willing to join;
  - passes the exams.
- **Reserve Force** (*Inkeragutabara*): the same general conditions, but with **A-Level completed**, and age limits set by qualification:
  - A-Level: **18–25**;
  - A0 degree or IPRC (*amashuri y'imyuga*): **not over 26** (*itarenze 26*);
  - specialists in Medicine, Engineering or Law: **not over 27** (*itarenze 27*).
- **Documents:**
  - national ID;
  - A-Level certificate (*A level Certificate*);
  - certificate of good conduct from the sector (*umurenge*) authority;
  - certificate of no criminal conviction.
- **Exams:** **2–17 June 2026, 08:00**, at district venues. Each district sits on two consecutive days.

## 2. RNP: Basic Police Course (2025)

- **Registration:** at the District Police Unit (DPU) of residence, **14 October to 7 November 2025**, 08:00–17:00 on working days.
- **Requirements:**
  - Rwandan;
  - **aged 18–25**;
  - in good health;
  - **A2** (six years of secondary);
  - good conduct, certified by local authorities;
  - **never sentenced to imprisonment of six months or more** (*kigeze cyangwa kirengeje amezi atandatu*);
  - never dismissed from public service;
  - ready to serve anywhere.
- **Documents:**
  - completed application form with a passport photo (from police.gov.rw);
  - copy of the national ID;
  - copy of the certificate;
  - good-conduct certificate from local authorities.
- **Contacts:** 0788311526, 0788311785.

## 6. Application forms (RNP Form 111, RCS recruitment form)

Both forms ask for the same identity block:

- family name, surname and other names;
- sex, national ID number, date of birth;
- father's and mother's names;
- marital status (RNP also asks for the number of children);
- nationality, telephone and email;
- **place of birth** and **place of residence**, each as village › cell › sector › district › province › country.

**Every identity field except telephone, email, residence and number of children is already in the NIDA identity record** (ADR-047 §1). The digital application should **pre-fill** them from NIDA, show them read-only with "as recorded by NIDA", and ask only for what NIDA does not hold.

Self-declared sections follow:

- physical fitness: chronic illness and injuries or defects. **These are health data:** DPIA, need-to-know, and the medical officer only.
- education history: school, dates, option, qualification and address. NESA and HEC verify the relevant level.
- **employment history**, with reasons for leaving;
- **"Have you ever been dismissed or asked to resign?"**;
- **"Have you ever been in security services?"**;
- hobbies (RNP), three references and supplementary details;
- a signed **declaration of accuracy**.

The RNP form also has an official block for the **District Recruitment and Selection Committee** (DPC, I/O, A/O, DJP and DCLO, with names, phone numbers and signatures). This is a staffing pattern for the venue roster (S09, P1).

The RNP brochure accompanying Form 111 states older conditions that **conflict with the 2025 announcements** (§8). The announcement for the campaign at hand governs.

## 8. Inconsistencies in the originals themselves (record; do not silently fix)

1. **RCS junior 2025:** the heading says exams run 26–31 August, but the district list dates them 25–29 August. Several districts sit on 25 August, the day after registration closes (24 August).
2. **RNP brochure vs the 2025 announcements:**
   - the brochure's cadet age is "not more than 35"; the 2025 announcement says 18–25;
   - the brochure's conviction bar is "more than six months" for both courses; the Basic 2025 announcement says "six months **or more**".

   The announcement governs its campaign.
3. **Walk-in registration on exam day** is announced by **RDF (2026)** and **RCS officer (2026)**. The platform treats walk-in as RDF-only (ADR-012). Allowing it for RCS is an owner/agency decision to record, not a default.

## 9. Policy facts the platform must model

- **Age is evaluated per category and per qualification within a campaign.** RDF Reserve has three caps; RCS officer has two tracks and a specialist cap.
- **Education alternatives** (A0 *or* A1 IPRC), and **minimum levels below A2** (RDF regular: S3).
- **Conviction rules differ by agency:**
  - RDF: never convicted;
  - RNP Basic: never sentenced to ≥ 6 months;
  - RNP Cadet: never sentenced to > 6 months;
  - RCS: never convicted *and* not under prosecution.

  The clearance check (ADR-048) must evaluate content against the campaign's rule.
- **Documents beyond registries:**
  - sector good-conduct certificate;
  - single-status certificate (RCS);
  - birth certificate (RCS officer);
  - notarisation (RCS);
  - government-doctor medical certificate (RCS).
- **Exam venues may serve several districts** (RCS officer), and one district can be split by date (RDF).
